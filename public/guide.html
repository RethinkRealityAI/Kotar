How to use Kotar Estimates

Everything the app does, in the order you would use it on a real job: build the estimate, send the client a link, handle their comments, get the approval. Screenshots are taken from the live app, so what you see here is what you will see on screen.

Guide updated with the latest release

What this is

Kotar Estimates replaces the Word document. You describe a job, pick a client, tidy the scope, set the price, and the app produces the branded estimate. Instead of emailing a PDF back and forth, you give the client a private link where they can read every line, comment on anything, download the PDF, and approve with their name. You see each step on your side.

It works in any browser on your computer or phone at kotar-estimates.netlify.app. The first time you open it, type the team passcode; the browser remembers it.

The dashboard

Four numbers at the top: how many estimates you have, how many are out with clients, how much money is waiting for approval, and how much has been approved. Below, every estimate with its status pill:

  • Draft - only you have seen it.
  • Sent - you generated a client link but the client has not opened it yet.
  • Viewed - the client has opened the link.
  • Approved - the client typed their name and approved.
  • Changes requested - the client left comments. See the Review tab.
  • Edited after approval - you changed an approved estimate; re-issue the link so they can approve the new version.

Use the filter chips or the search box (client, project, number) to find things. Click a row to open it.

Start a new estimate

  1. Press New estimate (top right).
  2. Choose a starting point. Bathroom loads your standard 11 sections exactly as written; Kitchen and Basement load their usual trades; Blank starts empty.
  3. The estimate number (KR-date) and today's date fill in automatically.

Everything saves as you type; there is no Save button. The small text under the estimate name says "Saved" or "Unsaved changes".

Describe the job (AI)

The fastest way to build an estimate. In the red Describe the job box, type (or press Talk and speak) what you would tell a colleague: the client and address, the room, what is new and what is being re-used, the price, and any conditions. Press Draft the estimate.

The app picks the right sections from your Scope Library, writes the lines in your usual wording, fills in the client, sets the price and HST, and adds your standard notes. It never invents a price: if you did not say one, the total stays $0 until you type it.

Changing an existing estimate: tick Apply as changes to this estimate and describe only what is different ("add a heated floor, $900 more"). Everything else is left alone.

If the box says the AI is busy, wait a minute and try again; the form below always works by hand.

Pick the client

In the Client card, choose a client from the list or press New to add one. Their name, address, email and phone fill the estimate. If the AI drafted a client you have not saved yet, press Save as client so they appear in the list next time.

Once a client is saved, the card shows a Client portal link: one page where that client can see every estimate you have sent them (see Client portal).

Scope of work

Each section is a list of lines. Click any line to edit it. Press Enter to add a line below, Backspace on an empty line to remove it, or use the + and x that appear beside a line. A line ending in a colon (like "Rough in and finish of:") becomes a sub-heading.

Under every section: Add line, Section below (insert a new section right there), and a note telling you whether the section still matches your library.

  • Matches library - same wording as your Scope Library.
  • Changed from library - you edited it here. Update library makes your library match; Use library version puts the standard wording back.
  • One-off section - not in the library. Save to library keeps it for future estimates.

Move sections with the up/down arrows; remove one with the trash icon (you can always add it back).

Adding sections

Press Add scope. Sections are grouped by category (Prep and Demo, Bathroom, Kitchen, Interior, Exterior, Systems, Finishing, Custom) with their standard lines underneath. Tick the ones you want and press Add; they drop into the right place in build order. Sections already on the estimate are marked "Added". Type in the search box to find a trade or a line.

For something not in the library, switch to New section: give it a name, a category, and its lines. Leave the checkbox off for a one-off; tick Also save to the Scope Library to reuse it later.

Pricing and notes

Single total is how your estimates have always been priced: type one sub-total and the app adds 13% HST. Price by section puts an amount box beside each section; the sub-total is their sum and the client sees a price per trade.

Notes and conditions print at the bottom of the estimate, one per line. Your three standard notes are there by default.

PDF

Press PDF in the top bar of any estimate to download the letter-size PDF with your logo, colours and HST number. The file is named "Kotar Estimate - Client - Date.pdf". Clients can download the same PDF from their link.

Generate the client link

  1. Press Generate client link. The link is copied to your clipboard and shown in a box.
  2. Send it however you like: Email it opens a pre-written email in your mail app, or paste the link into a text message. The app does not email anything by itself.
  3. Watch the Client link and approval card: Link generated, Opened by client, Approved (with the client's name and time).

The button changes to Copy client link once a link exists. Re-issue link resets the status to Sent after you have made changes; New link makes the old link stop working (use it if a link went to the wrong person).

$0 estimates: if you try to generate a link with no price, the app asks first. Add the price, or choose "Generate anyway" if you want the client to see the scope only.

What the client sees

No login, no app to install. The client opens the link and sees their estimate exactly as your preview shows it, with:

  • Approve this estimate - the total, a box for their full name, and a checkbox confirming they read the scope. The Approve button only lights up once both are done.
  • A comment button beside every line and section title. Pressing it opens a small box under that line. Their comments collect in the Your comments panel, where they can edit or remove them, add a general note, and press Request changes.
  • Download PDF and, if they have several estimates, All your estimates.

If they approve while they still have comments, the comments come through to you as notes attached to the approval. Comments they have not sent yet are kept on their device, so they can come back and finish later.

Review tab

When a client requests changes, the Review tab shows a badge. Each card lists the client's general note and every line comment ("Plumbing / New shower - Could this be a rain shower head instead?"). Tick a comment once you have dealt with it, or Mark all resolved.

Open estimate takes you to the builder, where each comment appears right under its line so you can change the wording or price there and press Resolve. When everything is resolved, press Re-issue client link: the client gets the updated estimate to approve, and their old comments move to history.

Client portal

Every client gets a portal link (it is created the first time you generate an estimate link for them; you can copy it from the Client card). It lists each estimate you have sent them as a card - project, number, total, and a plain-language status such as "Awaiting your approval" - with buttons to review, approve, or download. Useful for clients with more than one job, or who lose the original link.

Clients

Add a client once and pick them on any estimate. Click a card to edit details or add private notes (never printed). Estimates keep their own copy of the client details, so editing a client does not change estimates already sent.

Scope Library

The Scope Library is the wording behind the Add scope picker, the templates, and the AI. Every section is a card you can edit: rename it, move it to another category, add or reword lines, then Save changes. Built-in sections can be Removed from the picker (tick "Show removed sections" to restore) or Reset to default; your custom sections can be deleted. New scope section adds one directly.

Library changes apply to new estimates. Estimates you already made keep their own copy of every line.

Questions

The client says the link does not work.

Either you pressed New link (the old one stops working) or the estimate was deleted. Open the estimate and copy the current link from the Client link card.

I changed an approved estimate by mistake.

The status becomes "Edited after approval" so nothing is lost. Put it back the way it was, or re-issue the link so the client approves the new version.

Can two people use the app at once?

Yes. Everyone uses the same passcode and sees the same estimates, clients and library. The app refreshes statuses when you switch back to its tab.

Where is the data stored?

On the Kotar Estimates server, not on your computer. Clearing your browser only forgets the passcode.

Does the AI see my client's details?

Only the description you type in the box is sent to draft the estimate. Nothing is sent when you edit the form by hand.
